Website speed and SEO: INP on a phone, not a Lighthouse vanity score

Author: Marcin Bochenek · Updated: 2026-08-24

“Give us 100 in PageSpeed” is a bad brief. Google has measured experience for years, not a lab screenshot on a cable. For interaction, INP counts (not dead FID); for paint, LCP; for stability, CLS. Speed and SEO meet where a paid or organic click dies before the H1 promises anything. In the company-site package I promise on-page and mobile CWV — not a religion of green boxes without conversion.

INP instead of FID — so we speak the same language

FID left Core Web Vitals. If an agency in 2026 reports FID as success, it is auditing a memory. INP describes how the page reacts to tap and type. Heavy JS, hydrating a whole SPA, a chat jammed into the hero — INP killers. That is why brochures sit on a light stack (Astro/Next), not a theme with twenty “optimisation” plugins.

Lighthouse on desktop with a developer cache lies twice: device and network. I take decisions from the field (CrUX, if there is traffic) or from a real phone after deploy. Implementation has a “measure after launch” step for that reason.

Where speed is SEO, and where it is only conversion

Google uses experience signals as part of ranking, but you will not buy a position with image compression alone when nobody links and there is no intent-matched content. Conversely: a campaign that lands on a 6-second LCP burns budget regardless of title. That is bucket 2 in why the site does not sell.

  • Images: a modern format, dimensions, not a 4k PNG hero.
  • Fonts: preconnect, not five families “just in case”.
  • JS: do not load Three.js on a brochure “because it looks nice”. The game and hangar are separate entries.
  • Third-party scripts: every pixel is INP. Marketing consent is not free.
I do not publish a fake “PageSpeed 98 on Mint” here. You can measure Mint and Plumm yourself. iDrive is not live — there is nothing to boast about in production.

Crawlable HTML versus a hash SPA

This journal is static HTML in dist, not `/#artykuly`. That is an SEO decision, not a fashion. Hash routing is not an address for Search Console. If you want an organic channel, the URL must exist without JavaScript as the only content carrier. A brochure without a blog does not need that. Clusters do.

What I will not do for a score of 100

  1. I do not cut copy that sells so Lighthouse smiles.
  2. I do not leave a “fast” page without a form that works on slow LTE.
  3. I do not promise field CWV when there is no traffic yet — lab is a hypothesis.
  4. I do not sell a monthly “we optimise plugins” on a stack I do not ship.

On a B2B company website speed is in scope because the phone is where the decision happens. In the Launch package, path measurement joins: a slow payment step kills the funnel harder than three Lighthouse points.

Crawlable HTML versus a hash Google does not treat as an address

This journal is static HTML. That is an SEO decision, not a fashion. Hash routing is not an address for Search Console. If you want an organic channel, the URL must exist without JavaScript as the only carrier of meaning. A brochure without a blog does not need that. Clusters do. I do not confuse this with a “blog plugin in a page builder” that duplicates query params and returns 200 on empty facets.

Hreflang on the Ukrainian version is `uk` or `uk-UA`, not an invented `ua`. The URL prefix is `/ua/` because humans read it and it stays consistent with the homepage. Mixing UK (United Kingdom) with UA in the UI is a mistake I fix when I see it. Speed and i18n meet here: three copies are three LCPs, not one “translated div”.

What I will not cut so Lighthouse smiles

  1. Copy that sells. An empty hero with a 100 score collects no enquiries.
  2. A form that works on slow LTE. A “fast” site without a CTA is a measurement error.
  3. Proof you can click. A poster image is not Mint.
  4. Enquiry measurement. A pixel has an INP cost — that is why I do not bolt five “just in case”.

If you pay for traffic, we fix INP and LCP on the paid path first, not “the whole site to 100”. Why a site does not sell often starts in bucket 2: people arrive and leave because the first screen is heavy. A redesign without measurement is wallpaper.

The stack I use when speed is part of the offer

Astro or Next for a brochure, not WordPress from ThemeForest with twenty cache plugins fighting each other. Images in a sane format, no 4K hero video under the CTA. Limited fonts. Third-party scripts behind consent, not at start. This is not JAMstack religion. It is arithmetic: every kilobyte of courtesy to marketing is discourtesy to INP on a 2020 Android.

After implementation I leave you a way to measure, not a screenshot from launch day. Networks change, pixels get added, someone pastes Tag Manager “for a moment”. Speed and SEO are hygiene, not a closed project. If you do not want that hygiene, I do not promise rankings — and I do not own a campaign that burns budget on a spinner.

Questions

Does 100 in Lighthouse guarantee rankings?

No. That is a lab. Ranking is intent, content, links and field experience. 100 with no enquiries is a hobby.

Can WordPress be fast?

It can, with a discipline most themes lack. That is why I do not sell it as the default brochure stack.

Do animations always ruin INP?

Not always. Unthought JS on the critical path does. CSS decoration can be cheap. Three.js in a brochure hero — rarely.

When should we measure again?

After production deploy, on a phone, with real cache. Then after add-ons (chat, pixel, video). Each add-on is a new INP audit, not “it was green once”.

Does a Lighthouse 100 on localhost mean anything?

Little. After deploy I open the site on a phone, on a network I do not control, and I look at LCP and INP. CrUX when traffic exists. Lab without traffic is a hypothesis, not a certificate. I do not publish PageSpeed 98 on Mint — measure it yourself. FID left Core Web Vitals; reporting FID in 2026 is auditing memories. Chat in the hero, SPA hydration and a pixel “just in case” kill INP. The game and 3D hangar are separate entries, not brochure luggage.

No. Articles are static HTML under /artykuly/, /en/articles/ and /ua/statti/. A hash is not an address for Search Console.
