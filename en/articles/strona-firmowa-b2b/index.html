A B2B company website that collects enquiries, not just “exists”

Author: Marcin Bochenek · Updated: 2026-08-24

A B2B company website is not a conference slide deck. It has one job: a buyer should understand in a few minutes what you do, for whom, roughly what it costs, and how to reply. Below is the scope I ship in the Start package (from PLN 2,000) — and an honest line for when that site still will not sell, because the problem is the funnel, not the brochure.

What a B2B company website means in practice

In B2B nobody buys from the hero. They buy once they understand scope, risk and the next step. The B2B company website I build has five pages or fewer: home, offer, about, proof, contact. I do not add a “SEO blog”, extra languages or a CRM at this price — those are extras or a the Launch package.

Home must answer three questions without a treasure hunt: is this for me, what do I get, what do I click. The offer cannot be a list of “comprehensive solutions”. I state what is in, what is out, and the time horizon. Contact is a form with a notification, not a lone mailto that dies on mobile and leaves no trace.

Proof is the hard part if you refuse to lie. I do not paste fake reviews. If you have a live deployment, I link it. Publicly I show Mint Apartments and Plumm. iDrive is not live. Agentic OS is an internal tool. Less proof beats a theatre of stars.

What the Start package (from PLN 2,000) actually covers

In that range you get a brand-specific build, not a marketplace theme, up to five pages, a spam-protected form, on-page SEO (title, description, headings, sitemap, mobile speed) and enquiry measurement after launch. The stack is usually Astro or Next — not WordPress from ThemeForest plus a “SEO in one click” plugin.

If an agency promises a “B2B company website with a blog, AI and a shop for PLN 4,000”, it is not the same product. Polish 2026 public price lists often put brochure sites at 3–7k, with a median “company website” around 6k in some round-ups. My floor is higher on purpose: I do not sell a swapped logo on a stock theme.

What B2B will not forgive above the fold

The usual failure: the company describes itself, not the buyer’s decision. “We are a passionate, dynamic team” does not qualify a budget. An owner wants to know whether you can handle their volume, whether you enter their industry, and whether you vanish after the invoice. So the first screen carries location, working model (remote / on-site in the Tri-City), response time and a price floor.

  • One promise in the H1, not three parallel businesses.
  • A price or a range — hiding behind “individual quote” with no order of magnitude filters out serious firms.
  • A CTA that can be finished on a phone in 30 seconds.
  • A link to something that works in public. If you have none, write process and terms — do not invent case studies.

The second failure: five CTAs. Call, write, download a PDF, book a demo, visit the shop. On a B2B company website I keep one primary action — a 20-minute audit or a form — and one weaker one (email). The rest destroys measurement.

When a B2B company website stops being enough

A brochure collects enquiries when traffic is already warm: referral, LinkedIn, local Google, someone who already knows they need you. It fails when you pay for clicks, when the buyer compares three proposals, or when the decision needs calculation (booking, configurator, qualification). Then we do not “tweak colours”. We move to a conversion funnel.

A real example from my work, not a slide: Mint Apartments is not a brochure. The guest gets a live calendar and price, pays on the operator’s domain, and checks in without a desk. That is the Platform package (from PLN 25,000), not the PLN 2,000 Start package. A pretty lodging site without booking would still leak margin to the portal.

Hour and saving figures for Mint and Plumm are directional — they depend on volume. In the audit I count your case, not an internet average.

SEO on a B2B company website: the minimum that matters

I do not sell a rank for “services for companies”. I sell a page Google can parse: one H1, logical H2s, a unique title, canonical, sitemap, no accidental noindex, speed (LCP and INP, not a Lighthouse vanity score). Website speed and SEO is a separate piece — the rule here is simple: if the phone paint takes three seconds, part of paid traffic never sees the offer.

Expert articles like this one are a separate channel. The company-site package does not include them. If you want organic, we plan clusters and crawlable URLs, not a hash in an SPA. That is a product decision, not “we will add a blog plugin”.

How the 20-minute audit actually decides

I do not start with a moodboard. I ask where enquiries come from, how many there are, what happens after the email, and whether a site has any right to pay for itself. Two enquiries a quarter and no referrals: fix the channel before a redesign. Traffic and a silent inbox: we diagnose why the site does not sell.

  1. Twenty minutes: a funnel map on paper, not three weeks in a whiteboard tool.
  2. A package recommendation: Start, Launch, Platform — or do not implement.
  3. If the numbers work — a 90-day plan with a first measurable effect.

After invoices are paid, the code is yours. NDA is standard. I will not lock you into a panel you cannot export.

The B2B company website I ship does not promise three businesses at once. One promise, one reader, one next step. Location and working model sit up front, because a business owner should not guess whether I do on-site in the Tri-City and the rest of Poland remote. A price threshold too. Hiding “individual quote” with no order of magnitude cuts enquiries from people who will never enter the Start package (PLN 2,000) — and from those who will enter Platform (PLN 25,000) but refuse a call about a brochure.

Proof stays clickable or I skip the theatre. Mint and Plumm are public. iDrive is not live, Agentic is internal. Process and rules beat generated stars. If you have nothing to click yet, we write how I work and what I refuse — that is also an offer. A fake B2B case comes back in the first serious email.

When this site stops being enough and you need a funnel

When you pay for attention, when the decision needs qualification (volume, budget, number of properties) — that is the Launch package (from PLN 8,000). When booking or a panel must close on your domain — that is Platform (from PLN 25,000). Then the Start package (from PLN 2,000) is a bad purchase, because you bought a brochure to do a funnel’s job. Brochure or funnel and the Launch package exist so we name that line before the invoice, not mid-sprint. I do not bolt an “SEO blog” onto a brochure — that is a separate channel and separate crawlable URLs.

One CTA and one language — before someone adds “while we are at it”

A B2B company site in this budget has a right to be narrow. A blog, UA, CRM and chat “on the side” are separate invoices. Better five pages that collect enquiries than twenty nobody maintains. If after 20 minutes you hear you need a funnel — we do not bolt a section onto a brochure. We change the box. Hreflang and the /ua/ prefix are not a table decoration. They are an i18n decision I do not stuff into 8k. Proof: Mint and Plumm to click. The rest without generated stars.

Questions

Does a B2B company website need a blog?

No. A blog makes sense when someone will write regularly and URLs are indexable. The Start package does not include a blog — that is a separate decision, not decoration.

How many pages do I actually need?

Usually five or fewer. Every extra page without its own search intent dilutes authority and budget. One strong offer beats twelve thin tabs.

Will you build it in WordPress?

I do not sell a ThemeForest theme. For a B2B brochure I pick a light stack (Astro/Next), because a year later you count maintenance and speed, not plugin count.

When should I move from a brochure to a funnel?

When you pay for traffic, when you need qualification — that is the Launch package (from PLN 8,000) — or on-domain booking, that is Platform (from PLN 25,000), or when enquiry handling eats hours. Details are in the brochure-versus-funnel article.

How many pages actually fit in the Start package (from PLN 2,000)?

Up to five, scoped on the audit: home, offer, about, proof or work, contact. I do not add an SEO blog, extra languages or a CRM at that price. A form with notification, on-page, sitemap, phone speed, an upgrade path. Stack is usually Astro or Next. If someone promises B2B with a blog, shop and AI at the same price, you are comparing another box — that is the Launch package. PL/EN/UA and hreflang are extras — the UI flag is UA, HTML language is uk, not lang=ua.

What proof do you expect if invented reviews are forbidden?

Clickable proof. Publicly I show Mint Apartments and Plumm. iDrive is not live. Agentic is internal. If you have nothing to click yet, we write the process and what I refuse — that is also an offer. A hidden “individual quote” with no order of magnitude cuts serious enquiries.
